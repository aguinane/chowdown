title: Korean Fried Chicken

tags:
- "mains"
 
ingredients:
- "*some* gochujang"
- "*some* soy sauce"
- "*some* rice vinegar"
- "*some* honey"
- "*some* ginger"
- "*some* garlic"
- "*some* sesame oil"
- "*some* flour"
- "*some* cornstarch"
- "*some* salt"
- "*some* water"
- "*some* chicken"
- "*some* oil"
- "*some* sesame seeds"
- "*some* green onions"
 
directions:
- Start by making the sauce in a bowl. Combine gochujang, soy sauce, rice vinegar, honey, ginger, garlic and sesame oil in the bowl and whisk until smooth.
- In a separate bowl, add flour, cornstarch, and salt. Whisk to combine with water until a smooth batter.
- Cut up chicken into bite-sized pieces.
- Dip the chicken in the batter until all coated. Optionally add a little bit of the sauce to the batter, although most of it should be saved for later.
- Fry the chicken in oil until crispy.
- Dry some of the oil off the chicken with paper towels.
- Add chicken to a bowl and then pour the sauce over the chicken.
- Top with sesame seeds and green onions.
