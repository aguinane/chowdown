tags:
- "snacks"
 
ingredients:
- "*1* egg"
- "*1 tbsp* caster sugar"
- "*1 tsp* vanilla essence"
- "*3/4 cups* milk"
- "*1 cup* self-raising flour"
 
directions:
- Beat egg, caster sugar and vanilla essence
- Mix in milk
- Add in self-raising flour
- Spoon mixture into frying pan
- Cook until golden
- Serve with fresh fruit, honey and cream
