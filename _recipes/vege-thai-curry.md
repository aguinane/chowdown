title: Vege Thai Curry

tags:
- "curries"
- "veggie"
 
ingredients:
- "*some* onion"
- "*some* capsicum"
- "*3-4tbsp* red thai curry paste"
- "*some* green beans"
- "*1 can* coconut cream"
- "*some* rice"
 
directions:
- Saute onion and capsicum
- Add red thai curry paste and stir
- Boil green beans in microwave
- Add coconut cream
- Simmer for 10 mins or until less watery
- Serve with rice
