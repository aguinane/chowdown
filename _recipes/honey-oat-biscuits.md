title: Honey Oat Biscuits
image: honey-oat-biscuits.jpg
 
tags:
- "snacks"
 
ingredients:
- "*125g* butter"
- "*1/2 cup* white sugar"
- "*2 tbsp* honey"
- "*1 cup* plain flour"
- "*1 tsp* baking powder"
- "*1/2 tsp* cinnamon"
- "*1 1/2 cups* rolled oats"
- "*some* chia"
 
directions:
- Preheat oven to 180 degrees
- Line baking tray with paper
- Cream  butter, white sugar, honey together until pale and fluffy
- Add plain flour, baking powder, cinnamon, rolled oats and some chia seeds together until combined
- Place in fridge for 15 mins
- Roll into balls, flatten with floured fork
- Bake for 12-15mins or until golden
