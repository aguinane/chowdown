ingredients:
- "*4 cups* plain flour"
- "*1 tbsp* yeast"
- "*1 tbsp* sugar"
- "*1/2 tsp* salt"
- "*2 tbsp* olive oil"
- "*1/2 cup* warm milk"
- "*1/2 cup* warm water"
 
directions:
- Preheat oven to 90 degrees.
- Mix plain flour, yeast, sugar, salt in a bowl.
- Mix in olive oil, warm milk (or oat milk or water) and warm water until consistency is right. You can just use water if you want to make it vegan or dairy free.
- Turn the oven off. Make sure the oven is warm but not hot (approx 40°C) or it will kill the yeast.
- Knead for about 5 mins.
- Split into three and roll into bases (makes 3 or 4 depending on thickness)
- Spray bottom of bases with oil and prick top with fork.
- Leave bases to rise for about 30 mins.
- Put toppings on pizza and then cook!
