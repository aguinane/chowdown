ingredients:
- "*2 teaspoons* yeast"
- "*1/8 cup* sugar"
- "*3/4 cup* milk"
- "*1/4 cup* melted butter"
- "*1 egg* egg"
- "*2 cups* plain flour"
- "*1/2 cup* sultanas"
- "*1/2 cup* icing sugar"
- "*1/2 tblsp* butter"
- "*1/2 tblsp* milk"
- "*1/2 cup* coconut"
 
directions:
- Combine yeast, sugar and warm milk in a small bowl. Stand in a warm place for 10 minutes or until frothy.
- Add melted butter, lightly beaten egg and plain flour and mix.
- Mix in sultanas.
- Knead dough on a floured surface for 5 minutes or until smooth and elastic.
- Roll into 6 equal pieces.
- Leave in warm oven for 45 minutes to rise.
- Preheat oven to 200°C.
- Bake buns for 13 minutes.
- Make icing by mixing icing sugar and melted butter and milk. Add food colouring and coconut.
