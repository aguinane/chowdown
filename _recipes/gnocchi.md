tags:

ingredients:
- "*some* potatoes"
- "*some* salt"
- "*some* flour"
- "*some* water"
 
directions:
- Cut up and boil potatoes and then mash.
- Add salt, flour, and water and mix into a dough.
- Roll into thin strips and cut into small gnocchi shaped pieces.
- Pan fry or bake in the oven until golden brown.
