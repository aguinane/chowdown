title: Pumpkin and Quinoa Salad
image: pumpkin-and-quinoa-salad.jpg
 
tags:
- "salads"
 
ingredients:
- "*1 cup* rice"
- "*1 cup* quinoa"
- "*1/2* pumpkin"
- "*some* cranberries, @pepitas, @edamame beans"
- "*some* lettuce"
- "*some* green goddess dressing"
 
directions:
- Preheat oven to 180 degrees
- Cook rice and quinoa in pressure cooker for approx 15 mins
- Dice pumpkin and season with spices and olive oil
- Roast pumpkin in oven for approx 20-30mins until crispy
- Combine rice, quinoa, pumpkin, cranberries, @pepitas, @edamame beans, lettuce in salad bowl
- Drizzle salad with green goddess dressing
