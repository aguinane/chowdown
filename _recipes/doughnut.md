---
layout: recipe
title: Doughnut
source: https://letthebakingbegin.com/doughnuts/
size: 18
 
tags:
- "snacks"
 
ingredients:
- "*2½ tsp* yeast"
- "*2 tbsp* warm water"
- "*1 cup* warm milk"
- "*60 g* butter"
- "*3* eggs"
- "*2½ tbsp* sugar"
- "*1/4 tsp* salt"
- "*3¼ cups* plain flour"
- "*some* oil"
 
directions:
- Preheat oven to 90 C for rising later.
- Combine yeast and warm water in a bowl.
- Add warm milk, butter, eggs, sugar, salt
- Mix in plain flour.
- Knead the dough.
- Roll the dough and then cut into 18 pieces.
- Poke holes in the middle with a finger or knife.
- Let rise in the turned off oven for 1 hour.
- Shallow fry the doughnuts in oil for about 1 minute on each side.
