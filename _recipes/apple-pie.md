title: Apple Pie
image: apple-pie.jpg
 
tags:
- "desserts"
 
ingredients:
- "*4* apples"
- "*1/2 tsp* vanilla"
- "*1/4* brown sugar"
- "*1/4* white sugar"
- "*1/2 tsp* cinnamon"
- "*1/2 cup* plain flour"
- "*3/4 cup* oats"
- "*1/4* brown sugar"
- "*1/4* white sugar"
- "*1/2 cup* butter"
- "*1/8 tsp* cinnamon"
- "*1/4 tsp* nutmeg"
- "*1/2 cup* butter"
- "*2 tbsp* milk"
 
directions:
- Preheat oven to 160 degrees
- Grease pie dish
- Peel and diced apples
- Combine with vanilla, brown sugar, white sugar, and cinnamon
- Place in pie dish and flatten across the base
- Combine plain flour, oats, brown sugar, white sugar, butter, cinnamon, nutmeg
- Rub butter into mixture, add a dash of milk to moisten
- Pour over apple and pat down
- Place in oven for 30-35 mins or until golden
