title: Kimchi Fried Rice
source: https://www.recipetineats.com/korean-kimchi-fried-rice/
 
tags:
- "sides"
- "veggie"
 
ingredients:
- "*1.5 cups* rice"
- "*1 tbsp* sesame oil"
- "*3 tbsp* gochujang"
- "*3 tbsp* kimchi"
- "*1/2 cup* frozen corn"
- "*1/2 cup* frozen peas"
- "*3* eggs"
 
directions:
- Cook rice the morning or day before and put in fridge.
- Mix rice, sesame oil, gochujang, kimchi, frozen corn, frozen peas in a wok together and mix on medium heat.
- Add eggs and keep mixing until eggs are cooked.
